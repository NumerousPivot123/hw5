# Agent dashboard design

## Yale theme

We decided to make the dashboard Yale themed, in line with what we've done in the Campus Customs homeworks in the past. The whole page uses Yale blue, with Yale stripes in the background, to make it as Yale themed as possible.

## The bulldogs

The main fun thing we did is the bulldog design. Five Yale bulldogs represent the agents, and each one looks different:

| Bulldog | Agent | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Top | **Boss Agent** | Mortarboard and bow tie |
| Bottom | **Inventory Agent** | Beanie, carrying a shipping box |
| Bottom | **Accounting Agent** | Green eyeshade and round glasses |
| Bottom | **Facilities Agent** | Hard hat, with a key on its collar |
| Bottom | **Customer Service Agent** | Headset |

- **Layout:** the Boss Agent sits at the top, with lines connecting it to the four supporting agents below. The bulldogs and their labels are always on the page, between the tickets at the top and the database at the bottom.
- **Lighting up:** whenever an agent is called, its bulldog lights up in bulldog blue and bounces. When a supporting agent is working, the line between it and the boss lights up too. A speech bubble shows what the agent just did.
- **Celebration:** when a ticket is resolved or a payment approved, the whole team jumps.
- **Why:** this makes things more fun and interactive, because we can actually see the agents working in a fun way.

## Resolved tickets

Resolved tickets show up with a green check mark and an explanation of what the agents did, so they're easy to refer back to. A "Latest from the team" section and a recent activity feed show what the latest progress has been.

## Cash in the top right corner

The checking balance sits in the top right corner, so it's easily accessible to anyone visiting the site.

## The bulldog photo

We also threw in a bulldog photo at the very bottom of the page (Handsome Dan in his Y sweater, from the HW 4 site), just for fun, because who doesn't like looking at bulldogs?
